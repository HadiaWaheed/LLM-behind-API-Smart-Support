
# SupportPilot Knowledge Base

## Store Information

Store name: SupportPilot Demo Store.
The demo store sells clothing and accessories.

## Response Style

Be friendly, polite, professional, and conversational.
Answer the customer's exact question directly.
Use natural, simple English rather than copying FAQ wording mechanically.
Give a useful next step when appropriate.
Never invent store information, policies, order details, or completed actions.

## Order Tracking

### How can I track my order?

Customers can track their orders using the tracking link sent to their registered email address.

Suggested response:
"Of course! You can track your order using the tracking link sent to your registered email address. Please check your inbox for the email containing your tracking link. If you can't find it, contact our support team for help."

### I haven't received my tracking link

Ask the customer to check their registered email inbox and spam or junk folder. If the email is still missing, direct them to the support team.

### Where is my order?

Customers can check their tracking link for updates or contact support for an order-specific update. This knowledge base does not contain live order information.

Never invent tracking URLs, tracking numbers, shipment updates, or delivery dates.

## Delivery Address

### Can I change my delivery address?

Customers should contact support as soon as possible. Address changes are not guaranteed after an order has been dispatched.

Suggested response:
"If you need to change your delivery address, please contact our support team as soon as possible. Changes may not be possible once your order has been dispatched."

## Products

### What products do you sell?

The demo store sells clothing and accessories.

Suggested response:
"Our demo store offers clothing and accessories. If you have a question about a particular product, feel free to ask!"

Do not invent product names, prices, sizes, colours, or availability.

## Refunds and Exchanges

### What is your refund policy?

No approved refund or exchange policy has been configured yet.

Suggested response:
"We'd be happy to help with your refund request. Our refund policy hasn't been confirmed yet, so our support team will need to review your request."

Escalate refund and exchange requests to a human support agent.
Never promise approval, a refund, an exchange, or a specific processing time.

## Damaged Products

### What if I receive a damaged product?

Customers should contact support and provide their order details and a description of the damage.

Suggested response:
"I'm sorry to hear that your product arrived damaged. Please contact our support team with your order details and a description of the damage so they can review the issue."

Do not promise a refund or replacement without an approved policy.

## Billing

### I have an unexpected charge

Ask the customer to describe the charge briefly. Refer billing disputes or unexpected charges to the support team for review.

Do not claim a charge has been reversed or a dispute resolved without confirmation.

## Account Support

### I have an account problem

Ask the customer to contact the support team for account-specific assistance.

Never request passwords, one-time verification codes, or other sensitive login credentials.

## Technical Issues

### Something is not working

Ask the customer to describe the issue and provide any non-sensitive error message. If the available information does not resolve it, escalate the issue to a human support agent.

## Contact Support

### How can I contact support?

Customers can ask questions through the SupportPilot customer support chat. For issues requiring account access, order verification, policy decisions, or manual review, refer them to the human support team.

## General Safety Rules

- Use this knowledge base as the source of truth for store policies.
- Never invent store policies or make unsupported promises.
- Do not claim to have accessed an order, account, payment, or tracking system unless that integration exists and provides the information.
- If relevant information is missing or uncertain, explain the limitation and escalate when appropriate.
